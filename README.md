## 🛠️ Features & Architecture

### 1. Direct Whop Checkout Integration
* Integrated with live, verified Whop checkout plans supporting Apple Pay, Google Pay, and tokenized credit/debit card transactions.
* Bypasses broken iframe modal fallbacks with direct, reliable payment pathways.
* Eliminates checkout drop-off and unauthenticated session failures.

### 2. High-Ticket Contractor Pricing Architecture
* **Drone & Attic Inspection ($149):** Flat-fee diagnostic package featuring a 40-point aerial and attic thermal survey with storm damage reporting.
* **Roof Replacement Project Deposit ($500):** Initial project retainer securing crew scheduling and materials procurement on the contractor's calendar, with residual balance billed upon project milestones and final inspection.
* **Gutter & Flashing Tune-Up ($299):** Low-friction preventative exterior service package covering flashing resealing, apron inspection, and up to 50 linear feet of debris clearing.

### 3. Integrated Lead Intake & Pipeline
* Comprehensive on-site estimate request intake form capturing:
  * Full customer contact information (Name, Email, Phone, Address)
  * Estimated roof age and structural material
  * Scope of inquiry (Emergency leak repair, complete replacement, regular maintenance)
* Structured to pipe form submissions directly into the contractor's CRM workflow.

### 4. UI/UX & Responsive Optimization
* Mobile-first responsive layout with fixed sticky header navigation offset to eliminate anchor tag clipping.
* WCAG-compliant high-contrast typography and clear callout sections.
* High-trust contractor signals: Austin Metro badge, 24/7 emergency hotline call triggers, and full insurance/licensing verifications.
